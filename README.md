# A Plethora of Paladins for 1e FRPG, v0.2.0

An optional class module for the 1e FRPG system for Foundry VTT (system 0.40.0 or later). It adds a holy warrior for each alignment other than lawful good: the myrikhan (NG), garath (CG), lyan (LN), paramander (N), fantra (CN), illrigger (LE) and arrikhan (NE). In the article they are classes for non-player characters. Turn the module on or off in Manage Modules.

Based on Christopher Wood's article "A plethora of paladins" (Dragon #106, February 1986, p.45-56). The rules are restated in plain words; the numbers (experience, hit dice, spell and follower tables) follow the article. You need the article for its original text.

## Install

In Foundry's Setup screen, go to Add-on Modules, Install Module, and paste this manifest URL:

`https://github.com/gmjeremynow/frpg1e-paladins/releases/latest/download/module.json`

## What it adds

- Seven classes with experience, titles, hit dice (d6+3, d8, d12, d8+1, d10, d10, d8+1 up to each class's maximum, then fixed points), attacks per round, weapon proficiencies and penalties. They use the fighter's attack and saving throw tables, exceptional strength and the fighter's constitution bonus.
- Spells by each class's table: myrikhan and arrikhan (cleric, plus druid spells from the myrikhan's list), garath (cleric), illrigger (magic-user and cleric), lyan (any cleric or magic-user spell of 1st-4th level), paramander (own list, by intelligence) and fantra (own list). Wisdom bonus spells and spell failure where the class uses them.
- Compendia: a rules journal (tables, abilities, strictures, followers) and 313 spells, including the article's new spells: fire purge, read paramandic magic, cache, loyalty, spelltrap, azure flame, enchanted mirrors, question ball, slow mutation, and protection from plants, animals and insects.
- Turning undead for myrikhans and arrikhans; thief abilities at half level, without racial adjustments, for paramanders, illriggers and arrikhans.
- Attack dialog: the garath's bonus on every attack, the myrikhan's favorite weapon, and checkboxes for the myrikhan, lyan and arrikhan bonuses against their chosen foes.
- A Holy Warrior tab: hit dice, attacks, weapons, saving throw bonuses, percentage rolls (identifying plants and animals, the arrikhan's poisons and nightmare chance, the illrigger's assassination), followers with their tables, and the abilities and strictures.
- Record tab notes: ability minimums and maximums, race (human only), alignment, armor limits, druid spells off the myrikhan list, the arrikhan halfling and dwarf limits, the illrigger's 4th level spells.

## Choices made here

- The article gives no nonweapon proficiency slots, so they follow the paladin (Wilderness Survival Guide Table 1): 2 at first level and 1 more every 2 levels. Weapon slots follow the article.
- The lyan's 2/1 attacks are printed as "13 & up" while 3/2 runs to 14th; the module uses 2/1 from 15th.
- The paramander's apprentice stage (0 level, 2 hp, up to 1,172 xp) is not a class level here.
- The garath's 10th level is printed "18".
- Not applied automatically: saving throw bonuses against aligned magic, detections, protective auras, immunities, apprentices.
